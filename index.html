About M. Seb

Bonjour! I'm M. Seb. With over 30 years of dedicated teaching experience inside French classrooms, I have worked with hundreds of students who know grammar rules on paper but freeze when it comes to spontaneous conversation.

Traditional curricula often focus heavily on translation drills and written exams. My coaching bridges the gap between academic French and real, everyday fluency—teaching students how to think directly in French without translating word-for-word in their heads first.
